# Habesha TTS Streamer Partnership Proposal

## Page 1: Vision, Audience, and Value

### Purpose & Vision
Habesha TTS empowers Amharic-speaking streamers to monetize live communities through expressive text-to-speech overlays. Our partnership keeps streamers focused on storytelling while we handle payments, moderation, and infrastructure.

- **Revenue Share:** Streamers keep **60%** of every paid donation; Habesha TTS retains **40%** to fund hosting, moderation, compliance, and premium concierge support.
- **Why Now:** Growth-stage creators demand culturally relevant tools, fast payouts, and queue-based audio automation that works across Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, and Telegram.

### Target Audience
- Amharic or multilingual creators averaging 50+ concurrent viewers who need frictionless donation flows.
- Community hosts running charity drives, gaming tournaments, or educational live shows.
- Agencies managing multiple Ethiopian or diaspora creators seeking centralized payout operations.

### Core Value Proposition
1. **Instant Gratification:** Donations surface instantly in a real-time dashboard, prioritized queue, and branded notification sound.
2. **Trust & Safety:** Built-in anti-fraud scoring, profanity filters, and admin escalation keep shows family-friendly.
3. **Localized Engagement:** Full Amharic UI/UX, localized currency handling, and audience-friendly SSML voices.
4. **Operational Ease:** Zero-code onboarding via @HabeshaTTS_bot, automated receipts, and transparent ledger exports.

## Page 2: Program Snapshot & Economics

### Onboarding Flow
1. **Bot Registration:** Streamer shares Telegram handle → receives secure link UUID and onboarding checklist.
2. **Identity & Compliance:** Light KYB (government ID or business license) plus payout destination verification.
3. **Dashboard Activation:** Guided walkthrough covering donation controls, queue management, and notification sound selection.
4. **Go-Live Concierge:** 30-minute rehearsal session to calibrate volume, verify overlays, and rehearse moderation responses.

### Experience Highlights
- **Dashboard:** Real-time timeline, donation history, withdrawals, and admin chat.
- **Queue-based Playback:** Web Audio pipeline with manual skip, wake-lock, and connection monitoring.
- **Escalation Paths:** One-click summons for admin review, complaint resolution, or withdrawal clarification.

### Compliance & Monitoring
- KYB-lite identity checks (document + selfie) renewed annually.
- Automated anomaly alerts for rapid-fire donations, repeated chargebacks, or explicit text.
- Daily reconciliation against payment processor + Postgres ledger; manual overrides logged for audit.

### Revenue Model & Incentives
| Monthly Paid Donations | Streamer Share (60%) | Habesha TTS Share (40%) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Br 10,000 | Br 6,000 | Br 4,000 | Baseline tier |
| Br 25,000 | Br 15,000 | Br 10,000 | Unlocks priority support |
| Br 50,000 | Br 30,000 | Br 20,000 | Eligible for performance bonus (extra 5% rebate) |

- **Seasonal Boosts:** Holiday promo codes reduce platform fee by 5% for featured shows.
- **Referral Incentive:** Bring a new streamer who clears Br 15,000 in 30 days → referring streamer earns 5% bonus for three months.
- **Agency Bundle:** Agencies onboarding 5+ streamers receive consolidated reporting and custom success workshops.
