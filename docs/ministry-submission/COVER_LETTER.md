Honorable Minister / Director,

We respectfully submit this concept paper and prototype documentation for **HabeshaTTS**, a technology platform developed by young Ethiopian Computer Science graduates to empower Ethiopian content creators through AI-powered text-to-speech donation services.

### About Our Innovation

HabeshaTTS is Ethiopia's first real-time Amharic text-to-speech donation platform, enabling fans to send spoken messages to their favorite streamers during live broadcasts. Our platform:

- Leverages Google Cloud AI for natural Amharic speech synthesis
- Uses Telegram as a locally accessible donation gateway
- Supports Ethiopian payment methods (Telebirr, Chapa)
- Is **fully developed and operational**, ready for market launch

### Why We Are Approaching the Ministry

We have encountered a significant challenge: **Ethiopia's current business licensing framework does not have a category for "Creator Economy Platforms"** like ours. We are:

- NOT a traditional fintech company
- NOT a payment processor
- NOT a media/broadcasting company

We are a **digital platform that facilitates micropayments between fans and content creators** – a new category that exists globally (Streamlabs, Patreon, Ko-fi) but lacks regulatory definition in Ethiopia.

### Our Request

We humbly request the Ministry's support in the following areas:

1. **Regulatory Guidance** – Help us identify the appropriate licensing pathway or define a new category for Creator Economy Platforms
2. **Official Recognition** – Acknowledge HabeshaTTS as an innovative Ethiopian technology solution
3. **Payment Integration Support** – Facilitate access to official Telebirr and Chapa APIs
4. **Ecosystem Connection** – Connect us with incubators, accelerators, and government digitalization initiatives

### Our Commitment

In return, we commit to:

- Full compliance with all regulatory requirements
- Job creation for Ethiopian youth
- Knowledge sharing with the local tech community
- Transparent operations and reporting
- Supporting Ethiopia's Digital Ethiopia 2025 goals
